Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Who we are

RKMAN Creator Hub is a personal tool operated by Ritthikrai Sritep. Contact: Ritthikrai.sritep09@gmail.com

2. What data the Tool reads

After the account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access, the Tool may read: the account's basic profile identifier and display name (user.info.basic); follower, following, likes and video counts (user.info.stats); and the account's public videos with their title, cover image, share link and statistics such as views, likes, comments and shares (video.list).

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to display the account owner's own statistics in a personal dashboard. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than showing those statistics to the account owner.

4. Storage and sharing

Data is stored on the Operator's own device. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Access tokens are kept in a local file on the Operator's device and are not published.

5. This website

This website is static. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The sign-in callback page only displays the authorization code from the address bar in your browser and does not send it anywhere.

6. Your choices

You can withdraw the Tool's access to your TikTok account at any time in the TikTok app's security and permissions settings. To ask for stored data to be deleted, email Ritthikrai.sritep09@gmail.com.

7. Children

The Tool is not directed at children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.
