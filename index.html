RKMAN Creator Hub

A personal dashboard for a single creator.

RKMAN Creator Hub helps one creator track how their own short videos perform and which promoted products bring results. After the creator signs in with their own TikTok account, the tool reads the creator's public video list and basic statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, follower and video counts) and shows them next to the creator's own notes on each promoted product.

The tool is operated by its owner for their own account. It does not post, comment, follow or message on TikTok, and it does not read private data.
